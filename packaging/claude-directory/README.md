# GetWhys

GetWhys grounds your go-to-market work in what real buyers said. With this plugin, Claude checks your organization's GetWhys buyer interviews, personas, brand voice, and messaging frameworks before it writes or answers, instead of guessing at pain points, quotes, or sentiment.

Use it for:

- Writing or rewriting marketing content: blog posts, emails, landing pages, ads, sales decks, one-pagers, and RFP responses
- Buyer and market research: pain points, objections, win/loss reasons, and buying committees
- Competitive analysis, battlecards, and talk tracks
- Building personas and ideal customer profiles
- Reviewing a website or scoring a draft against your buyers and messaging

## What you need

A paid GetWhys account. The plugin includes the GetWhys connector. Connect it from the plugin's Connectors tab and sign in with your GetWhys account.

## What the plugin sends

The plugin's skill calls the GetWhys connector at api.getwhys.io. It sends your questions and the drafts you ask it to score to your organization's GetWhys workspace, along with a short note from Claude on why it made each call, and reads back interview evidence, personas, brand voice, and messaging. When you ask it to review a website, Claude reads those pages with its own web tools. The plugin sends nothing anywhere else, and it runs no code on your computer.
